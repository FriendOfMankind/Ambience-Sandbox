# How ambient is made and played live (research, 2026-09-30)

What working ambient artists use and do, especially live and improvised, and what that means for Tarn. Gear popularity comes from artist interviews, crowdsourced gear lists (Equipboard) and forum consensus. It is informed opinion, not market data.

## 1. The core idea: play little, let the system compose

Across very different artists, the pattern is the same: a simple input goes into a **processing system** (loops, long delays, reverbs, granular), and the system builds the piece.

- **Frippertronics (Fripp & Eno, 1970s).** Two reel-to-reel machines. Sound is recorded on the first, played back on the second, and fed back to be recorded again, so each pass layers over the old ones while they slowly degrade. Fripp performed this solo in pizza parlours and barber shops. ([Looper's Delight](https://loopers-delight.com/tools/frippertronics/frippertronics.html), [Valhalla DSP](https://valhalladsp.com/2010/05/26/early-tape-loop-experiments-with-eno-fripp-terry-riley-and-pauline-oliveros/))
- **Eno, *Music for Airports*:** loops of incommensurate lengths, so the combinations never repeat. (Already in Tarn as Rhythm → loops.)
- **Basinski, *The Disintegration Loops*:** old tape loops played until the oxide flaked off; the **decay itself is the composition**. Widely acclaimed. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Disintegration_Loops))
- **Julianna Barwick:** voice, mic and a looper (Boss RC-50). Every vocal loop is built live, mostly improvised, layer upon layer of reverbed voice. She later added a sampler, keys and a Moog. ([Roland](https://articles.roland.com/a-few-minutes-with-julianna-barwick/), [Tiny Mix Tapes](https://www.tinymixtapes.com/features/julianna-barwick), [The Quietus](https://thequietus.com/articles/13245-julianna-barwick-live-review))
- **Chihei Hatakeyama:** guitar through a looper (EHX 2880), tape echo (Strymon Volante), reverbs (Eventide Space, Strymon BigSky/blueSky) and Chase Bliss Lossy. **The reverb and looper stay fixed; he changes the modulation and delay during a piece.** He also mics acoustic guitar and processes it on a mixing console until it no longer sounds acoustic. ([Equipboard](https://equipboard.com/pros/chihei-hatakeyama), [TOKION](https://tokion.jp/en/2021/06/04/ambient-and-jazz-or-improvisation-part1/), [Fluid Radio](https://www.fluid-radio.co.uk/2019/10/conversation-with-chihei-hatakeyama/))
- **Kaitlyn Aurelia Smith:** Buchla Music Easel as an instrument she knows "like an extension of my brain". Her voice is processed through the system, and MIDI from the Easel triggers other sounds. She works with "intention and happy accident". ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/kaitlyn-aurelia-smith-interview), [RBMA](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2016/01/kaitlyn-aurelia-smith-interview/))
- **Suzanne Ciani:** Buchla modular played live in **quadraphonic** sound. Buchla's 227 module moved sound around the room under voltage control, so space is part of the performance. ([Soundbleed](https://soundbleed.org.nz/SUZANNE-CIANI-KRAUS), [Kennedy Center](https://www.kennedy-center.org/video/millennium-stage/contemporary-music/2018/dcdit-presents-suzanne-ciani-direct-current---millennium-stage-march-16-2018/))
- **Hainbach:** 1950s lab test equipment (a 26 kg sine generator), tube synths and tape loops; he likes instruments he has to "struggle with". ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainbach_(musician)), [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/hainbach-i-feel-i-make-better-music-when-i-have-to-struggle-with-an-instrument))
- **Stars of the Lid:** beatless drones of effects-treated guitar with piano, strings and horns, built from **volume swells** and feedback. ([starsend.org](https://www.starsend.org/sotl.html))
- **Harold Budd with Eno (*The Plateaux of Mirror*):** "soft pedal" piano, with notes suspended in long treated reverb. ([Slowist](https://slowist.org/2020/10/12/harold-budd-soft-pedal-composer/), [Progrography](https://progrography.com/harold-budd/review-harold-budd-brian-eno-ambient-2-the-plateaux-of-mirror-1980/))

## 2. Common sources (instruments)

Guitar (swells, EBow, open strings), voice loops, soft piano, analogue and West Coast synths (Buchla, Moog, Juno-type pads, OP-1), generative modular, strings and horns, field recordings, sine and test oscillators, and tape loops of anything.

**Generative modular:** random sources (Music Thing Turing Machine, Mutable Instruments Marbles) go through a **quantiser** so random notes stay in a scale. Slow cycles and attenuated voltages give forms that evolve slowly, and patches can run hands-off. ([MOD WIGGLER](https://modwiggler.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=202679), [Computer Music](https://www.pressreader.com/australia/computer-music/20220323/282746295250010))

## 3. The effects chain pros reach for

| Role | Typical gear | What it does musically |
|---|---|---|
| Looper / sound-on-sound | Boss RC-series, EHX 2880, Chase Bliss Blooper, Ditto | Builds layers live; overdub with decay |
| Tape echo | Strymon Volante / El Capistan, real tape | Long, darkening, wobbly repeats with high feedback |
| Big reverb | Strymon BigSky (Cloud, Bloom, Shimmer), Eventide Blackhole/Space, Valhalla Supermassive/Shimmer | Huge tails, **freeze/infinite**, pitch-shifted shimmer ([Gearspace](https://gearspace.com/threads/any-software-reverb-comparable-to-big-sky-reverb-you-enjoy-with-your-hardware-synths.1137085/), [Elektronauts](https://www.elektronauts.com/t/best-reverb-for-creating-soundscapes/141877), [Valhalla](https://valhalladsp.com/shop/reverb/valhalla-supermassive/)) |
| Granular | Hologram Microcosm (about 60 s of memory), Chase Bliss Mood, Make Noise Morphagene, Mutable Clouds/Beads | Clouds of grains from what you just played, re-pitched and smeared ([Hologram](https://www.hologramelectronics.com/pages/microcosm)) |
| Tape degradation | Chase Bliss Generation Loss, Lossy | Wow (slow pitch drift), flutter (fast), dropouts, hiss, filtering: "a lifeless pad feels alive" ([ANR review](https://audionewsroom.net/2024/03/beauty-in-imperfection-chase-bliss-generation-loss-mkii-review.html), [Northern Valley Audio](https://www.northernvalleyaudio.com/blog/lofi-tape-saturation-production-guide)) |
| Extreme stretch | Paulstretch / PaulXStretch | Stretches any sound up to 10,000× into a drone ([polarity](https://polarity.me/posts/articles/2026-07-07-paulxstretch-paulstretch-explained/)) |
| Space | Quad panning (Buchla 227), slow auto-pan | Sound moves around the listener |

## 4. Harmony and form

- **Voicings:** add9, sus2, sus4, maj7 and quartal (stacked fourths) chords, open strings and pedal-tone drones. These avoid the pull of plain triads. ([Guitar Woodshed](https://guitar-woodshed.com/ambient-guitar-chords-elevate-your-skill-now-free-lesson/), [North Coast Synthesis](https://northcoastsynthesis.com/news/ambient-chord-progressions/))
- **Slow harmonic rhythm:** often a single chord with changing voicings, or progressions that avoid landing on the tonic.
- **Swells instead of attacks:** volume pedal or EBow, sounds that fade in.
- **Form by accumulation and decay:** layers build (sound-on-sound), then thin or disintegrate. Long arcs of 10–40 minutes; change one thing at a time.
- **Live improvisation:** start with a drone or texture, add loops, keep most settings fixed, and perform a few (Hatakeyama: delay and modulation), then let it decay.

## 5. What Tarn has, and the gaps

**Already in Tarn:** drone (just-tuned), pad, bowls, plucks, keys, choir, beat; an FDN reverb, a ping-pong delay and an octave shimmer; Eno-style loops; Breath; just intonation; Blend with ambience.

**Gaps, in order of how much they'd move it towards "pro live ambient":**

1. **Sound-on-sound looper (Frippertronics):** capture the music bus into a long delay (8–30 s) with feedback, degrading each pass (darker, slightly wobbly). Dials: *Layers* (feedback) and *Decay* (loss per pass). The single most defining live-ambient process, and Tarn doesn't have it.
2. **Tape "Age":** wow, flutter, saturation, hiss and occasional dropouts on the music bus (Generation Loss, Basinski).
3. **Voicings and a pedal tone:** chords are currently stacked thirds only. Add sus2/add9/quartal choices and a sustained bass pedal under chord changes.
4. **Granular "Texture":** grains from the last few seconds of music, re-pitched and spread (Microcosm, Clouds).
5. **Performance gestures:** *Freeze* (hold the reverb or looper infinitely) and *Swell* (fade-in attacks for keys and plucks).
6. **Soft piano voice** (Budd): felt-piano tone with long release, the missing "hero" melodic instrument.
7. **Motion in space:** slow orbiting pan of drone partials and bowls (Ciani).
